# The Malefic Veil — City Miasma, Crude Settlement Index, Full Progression and Cooperative Trade

**Design contract amendment • 21 October 2026.** Architecture, data catalogs and host-side policy models exist in Java, but the world-streamed construction, actual proxy-geometry instancing, living farms, trade ledger, level upgrades, aircraft, ordnance and volumetric miasma rendering are **not** playable systems yet. Retain exact distinctions throughout release reports.

## 1. Permanent city miasma — resource sanctuary against aerial denial

Every generated city has a towering **billowing purple/black miasma** visible long before approach. This is not ordinary fog, a visual-only debuff, or a removable aircraft upgrade hurdle. It exists specifically to **preserve unavoidable ground-level expeditions into the cities for unique salvage and bulk METAL_STOCK/MACHINE_PARTS**. No faction may build aircraft, aircraft artillery, bomber platforms or airborne supply extraction inside it.

**Hard authoritative geometry:** `CityMiasma` emits one concentric air-denial **vertical column per generated city**, centered on that city's procedural center and sized to enclose the complete irregular/square footprint plus eight **macro parcels** of defensive standoff: radius `hypot(radiusX,radiusY)+8` in the *current macro blueprint only*. Derive a correctly measured high-resolution 3D world boundary when streamed continent units become real. The restriction spans from terrain to **above the aircraft flight ceiling**; high-altitude flight, descent below the visible cloud, teleport, respawn, entering by chunk loading, cockpit reconnect and fast tick tunneling are prohibited. `forbidsFlightSegment` checks swept movement against all city fields, not merely the two endpoint positions. All nine faction aircraft follow the same constraints; no faction-specific bypass, elite pilot immunity, cloaking or late upgrade eliminates a field.

**Aerial weapon envelope:** the final aircraft physics/ordnance server must also reject or harmlessly dissipate air-launched bombs, guided munitions, drones, bombardment, air-dropped cargo and damaging splash that would enter a city's protected resource-play area from outside. Otherwise circling at the rim would recreate the very resource denial this system prevents. Ground combat, ground players, neutral NPCs, salvage yards, trucks, city freight shipments and intercity highways **do not inherit any miasma movement ban**. Nothing in the miasma forbids foot/truck entry into city resource parcels.

**Visual plan:** Three low-poly/pixel-styled cloud strata—dark-violet grounded haze, mobile plum billows, distant luminous lilac canopy—using seed-stable layered 3D noise and scrolling shader samples (or bounded low-poly cloud-shell proxies in software renderer). Dense near silhouette, readable far-city skyline, slow wind shear, occasional eldritch pulse, minimal particle budget, LOD/impostor strategy. Aircraft HUD must show city-airspace exclusion rings, fog wall distance and a clear warning before entering; autopilot diverts/holds safely, no invisible surprise instant-kills. Testing needs two render modes, night visibility, 320x200 legibility, and server/client boundary parity.

**Anti-grief:** cities remain contested ground scavenging zones but cannot be shut down by a faction's endgame bomber. Aircraft can contest *wilderness* routes, workshops and fortresses; resource runs still require vehicles, guards, escort convoys or stealth ground traversal. Swooping above the cloud to fire into resource sites is also denied.

## 2. Construction index: crude geometry first, finished faction art later

Use the same practical source-of-truth-first philosophy as crude weapons: **every construction blueprint must have a stable immutable template ID, function, economy prerequisites, a footprint, interaction points, collision/load limits, a crude readable proxy, upgrade level and planned faction-specific art**. The primitive index is `CrudeStructureIndex`, generated from the **full** `CraftingEraRegistry.facilities()` rather than a hand-maintained partial list; `SettlementAuthority` now also indexes normal facilities from the full registry. `palisade-section` stays a later separate modular-wall budget instead of consuming an entire factory-sized facility slot.

Planned crude forms include framed tents and plank huts, raised crop beds, fenced pens, stake-wall panels, storage stacks/cellars, barrel/cistern shells, stone hearths, watch platforms, chimney buildings, machine frames, pipeworks, transport sheds, foundries, ceremonial altars, biological nests and graveyard monuments. These are **proxy assembly directions**, not a claim that they have already been rendered or successfully interacted with in a running game. Do not place entire paid art source archives in GitHub. Replacement with verified three-faction PSX models must preserve authoritative footprint and functional IDs.

**Construction progression:** `PLANNED → RESERVED → FOUNDATION → FRAME → COMMISSIONING → OPERATIONAL → UPGRADE_WORKSITE → OPERATIONAL`, or `DAMAGED / DISABLED / DESTROYED`. Materials, power, labor, clocks, parts and transport are real. Power/food/storage/production must be connected physically, never magically global. The founder's `SettlementAuthority` ACL, faction rules, capacity and protected wilderness geometry all must approve an operation before item debit.

## 3. Early survival, farming, food and first industry

`CraftingEraRegistry` schema **v2** adds these stable physical blueprint IDs and ordered new processing recipes, on top of the existing seven-era production registry. Advanced steps must remain **real worldsourced**: collecting edible seeds and soil, watering, crop growth, ploughing and harvesting require actual plots, climate, tool action and elapsed time. A recipe catalog is not automated live food.

| Era | Crude-to-refined structures | Output, survival service, upkeep |
|---|---|---|
| **E0 Stranded** | hand-tied campfire, tent kit, crate, short fence, stake trap, noisemaker, simple hand filter and workbench | limited warmth, basic cover, drink/cook, wound dressing and first tools; no claim and no electricity |
| **E1 Encampment** | Claim Heart, field shelter, raised garden, seed nursery, compost yard, herb plot, root cellar, smokehouse, fiber loom, cooking hearth, rain catcher, dry storage, watch post, wooden palisade | irrigated staple foods, compost, dried food, seed propagation, cloth, finite clean water, medicinal herbs, cold/dry storage |
| **E2 Salvage** | charcoal kiln, stone furnace, forge, freight bay, grain mill, irrigation channels, animal pen, recycler | meal sacks, stored grain, brick/iron bloom, essential machinery/parts, farming tools and repaired scavenged vehicles |
| **E3 Steam** | steam boiler, machine shaft, metal press, conveyor, fixed pipes/water movers | drive gears, advanced machine frames, bulk grain/flour handling, fuel/water logistics |
| **E4 Electricity** | generator, electric bus, precision lathe, automatic sorter, water works, fuel refinery, controlled greenhouse | power, controller boards, filtered process water, fuel and higher harvest reliability with real energy draw |
| **E5 Complex Industry** | industrial workshop, motor pool, assembly line, supply depots, command/research halls | serviceable vehicle parts, plant equipment, standardized ammo, repair and shipping contracts |
| **E6 Incursion** | expensive faction divergent works, monumental graveyard, aircraft facilities, high-defense ammo specialty plants | endgame machines and level-50 modernization, grave recovery and air warfare; profound imported-resource dependence |

**Farmer UX:** plot planting requires seeds/cuttings; each plot has a crop species, local fertility, season, moisture, disease/pest status, growth phase, expected yield, storage destination and harvest eligibility. Soil amendments are consumed; overfarming depletes fertility until compost/fallow/restoration is applied. Crop output is bounded by real inputs and time. Greenhouses convert power/fuel into reliability but cannot conjure water/seed stock. Livestock pens eventually require actual persistent animals, feed, veterinary care and breeding; merely building a pen does NOT manufacture hides or meat. Herbs have separate propagation and decay characteristics. Perishables spoil differently in crates, cellars, smokehouses and cold storage.

**Survival equipment:** staged hand-gathered cloth and leather/organic substitutes, water skins and canteens, cooking pot, bedroll, bandages, filters, durable tools, backpacks, tents, storage crates, field repair kit, vehicle repair kit, industrial protective gear and late insulated/hazmat/biological equivalents. Respect carried inventory mass, death loss, actual workstation type, durability and game-time use. New recipes become truly usable only when `SurvivalCrafting.Item`, container persistence, model IDs and server transaction tests are updated; the industrial registry alone does not grant items.

## 4. Every object can reach level 50—without making obsolete items free or immortal

The design-side `EquipmentUpgrades` defines a **one-step** modernization contract across facilities, defensive towers and vehicles. The cap is exactly **50**, bound by the current founder/owner level, era research, actual materials and work time. Different items need different parts/fuel/materials and increasingly demanding stations; prices scale upward. Saving/upgrading never duplicates an existing stockpile, fuel, structure occupancy or deployed unit. Claim Heart and graveyard keep their special slot rules. No cheap level-1 recipe may be used to jump directly to level 50.

| Levels | Physical modernization | Materials / dependencies | Why it stays useful |
|---:|---|---|---|
| **1–9** | Crude | timber, stone, fiber, manual labor | basic warmth, harvest, storage, protection and crude vehicle repairs |
| **10–19** | Reinforced | billet, fasteners, salvaged parts | sturdier walls/frames, bigger crop yield/storage, reliable machinery |
| **20–29** | Mechanized | gear trains, smelted stock and power transmissions | mechanized throughput, better turret feed, drive-train reliability |
| **30–39** | Electrified | boards, precision modules, powered service | controlled production, targeting, refrigeration, fuel economies |
| **40–49** | Industrial | assembly controllers, specialist components, qualified support | powerful specialized outputs, upgrades to advanced defenses/vehicles |
| **50** | Fully specialized | E6 high-tier components and massive costs | ultimate performance and **higher upkeep**, not zero maintenance or immunity |

**Effect rules:** a level-50 turret is useful against appropriate targets without overcoming designed armor/evasion counters. Defensive range/tracking/damage and ammo draw are bounded and specialized; a rapid light anti-air gun must not become a universal large-aircraft killer. A level-50 generator produces more reliable power at greater wear/fuel/cooling cost. A level-50 farm produces more with finite water, nutrients and labor. A level-50 truck improves load handling/parts/armor but still pays fuel, cargo mass and repair; a high-level aircraft still obeys city miasma, anti-air balance and endgame upkeep. Levels scale **efficiency**, but upkeep ramps faster at the high end, encouraging division of labor.

**Entity persistence:** stable structure/vehicle ID, original template ID, explicit level, upgrade-in-progress stage, reserved materials, damage/wear, faction look, owner character ID, electrical/production network IDs, authoritative next-service tick and revision. Upgrade transactions must be atomic, authorized and uniquely identified; restart at every phase must not double charge or duplicate output. A level-50 structure still occupies its one normal facility or defensive slot.

## 5. The fortress specialization constraint — trade as a necessary operating condition

The maximum founder settlement allowance stays **28 normal facilities**, **10 automated defenses**, **6 production queues** and **2 extension pylons**, plus the separate approved Claim Heart and graveyard allocation. A defense is both a dedicated automated-defense slot and a real physical power/ammo consumer; generic factory/production structures consume the **28 normal facility** budget.

A successful fortress at level 50 should be possible, but a single fortress may NOT simultaneously run all 10 top-level defenses on its own power and produce their entire ammunition/repair/food supply. Supply shortages lead to normal graceful degradation (priority/shed/pause, visible queues and finite reserve), not instant base deletion or secret ammo creation. The global faction alliance rule remains hard: same-faction crews always allied in combat, while their independently owned settlements can make competing commercial contracts.

`SettlementSupplyBalance` is the initial deliberately simplified **one-hour feasibility model**. Eight normal slots are reserved for irreducible command/security/storage/freight/water/workshop/maintenance and research/communications support, leaving **20 normal facility slots** for high-end power and munition production. Optimistic *fully upgraded* power plant cap: 45 power units/hour; specialist ammunition plant cap: 4 standardized supply units/hour. Ten level-50 defenses require **320 power units/hour and 70 ammunition units/hour**. To produce 320 local power requires at least **8 generators**, leaving 12 plant slots for a theoretical maximum **48 ammo units**, a **22-unit ammunition import deficit every operating hour**. Even before food, spare parts, fuel, losses, power-plant fuel, or other needs, the full fortress is mathematically incapable of self-production. Exhaustive allocation test covers every combination of the 20 remaining slots.

This is a **tuning/invariant baseline**, not currently measured live consumption. Later real recipe graph and grid must preserve the impossible-solo/full-defense inequality without fake offscreen resource drains. Bases with fewer/lower defenses can support themselves for reasonable early play. Remote support requires **physical hauled goods** and signed contract receipts; bare player presence or shared faction ID does not conjure stocks.

Maximum-defense operator example: fortress owns 8 power plants, 12 ammo cells plus core support and buys the missing 22+ supply units plus fuel/food/service components from other settlements. A neighboring agricultural settlement optimizes food and chemical inputs. A specialist ordnance factory makes advanced ammunition for multiple forts. A separate refinery/transport hub handles fuel and bulk components. None may occupy or build over protected cities to shortcut salvage hauling.

## 6. Three faction production systems—different goods AND different cooperation patterns

Same-faction players and crews are always allies, **not** globally co-owners. Independent same-faction settlement IDs provide production receipts; standard multiplayer does not require a suicidal trade with an enemy faction to progress. Optional cross-faction black markets are hazardous future gameplay and never substitute for each faction's complete legitimate supply path.

### The Unraveled — stable serial industrial freight chain

The Unraveled need **batch-consistent materials, manufacturing tolerances and heavy logistics**, favoring reliable stores and big convoys. Different factories specialize: extraction and ore sorting → metallurgical refinement → electronics / machined feed lines → final tested ordnance. Iron/alloy and electronic branches must converge. Intermediate goods are relatively durable, bulky and expensive to move, so **vehicles, freight receiving, maintenance, roads and convoy escorts** are essential. Contracts specify delivered quantity/grade, damage allowance, shipment receipt, manufacturing completion and storage. Standardized goods permit slower, less synchronized cooperation, but require more physical mass and sequential heavy industry. Trade commodities: sorted ore, hardened alloy, tracking circuits, precision feedlines, ballistic supply.

### The Penitent — parallel offerings and timed ceremonial convergence

The Penitent require **separate ritual actors and ceremonial sites**: votive incense/offering preparation, reliquary seals, several simultaneously participating choir operators and a consecrating/threefold rite before consecrated munitions are valid. Stages branch and converge rather than resembling one regular factory assembly line. Some intermediate blessings are **perishable or time-locked**, not safely stockpiled forever. Contracts require named output reservations, site readiness, participant count, ceremonies within declared windows and actual consumption of physical reagents. If one site misses the window, stocked metal remains but transient resonance expires; nobody loses all materials due to a network tick failure. Trade commodities: incense, witness seals, chorus resonance, bound liturgy, sacred supply.

### The Begotten — biological branching, maturation and perishable living transport

The Begotten build **ecological production networks**. Garden/nutrient colonies and independent spore culture sites supply incubation; compatible feed and cultures grow chitin; another symbiotic colony synthesizes binding resin; only mature stocks are assembled into living ammunition/organic machine organs. Resource lots have **viability, age, contamination, environmental temperature/moisture, compatible lineage and spoilage windows**. Growth takes genuine elapsed time and consumes nutrients and water throughout, with bounded offline advancement. Trading requires live containers, feeding/transport conditions and physical handover; a delivered dead culture is not a valid manufactured part. Trade commodities: growth feed, viable spores, grown chitin, binding resin and spine supply. Their production graph is a branching living ecosystem rather than an instant resource converter.

`FactionTradeNetworks` defines five or more stage descriptors per faction, four distinct production specializations per faction, different methods (`FREIGHT_ASSEMBLY`, `TIMED_RITUAL`, `LIVING_MATURATION`) and a **minimum three independent settlement-origin receipts** for endgame final batches. This is a **planning/provenance gate**; caller-supplied map strings currently do not prove ownership, content authenticity, cargo transfer or payment. Future server transactions must issue signed/ledgered input lots under distinct verified settlement founders, follow the component DAG and verify actual batch work, operator participation, timestamps and consumable inventory before minting an output. These proofs cannot be replaced by a client packet claiming three bases.

### Fairness without sameness

Compare faction production on **actual labor-hours, founder settlement infrastructure costs, expected loss, time-to-first-supply, transport distance, fuel/power costs, defense output/hour and recoverability after raids**, not on making every recipe take the same minutes or three trivial token items. Unraveled mass/convoy bottleneck, Penitent synchronization/time-window bottleneck and Begotten maturation/decay bottleneck must each be hard in *different ways*. Failure is signposted and repairable, never invisible RNG deletion. Another faction cannot gain a free version of advanced items merely by choosing a different sprite.

## 7. Player trade, physical logistics and server trust

**Settlement-to-settlement contracts:** issued by authorized founder/Quartermaster, identified by creator, recipient, faction, revision, goods, actual source lot, quantity/quality/condition, unit of account/payment, origin/destination storage, capacity reservation, deadline and cancellation/refund rules. The accepted deal creates an escrow/reservation, not free transferred stock. Drivers physically move barrels, crates, machinery pallets or living cargo by road; destination confirms unload; only then do inventories/credits change atomically. Damage, theft by hostile faction, spoilage, lost power, broken vehicles, disconnected couriers, lapsed ritual windows and partly delivered cargo all have consistent visible outcomes and auditable incident ledgers.

**Markets:** Unraveled favor bulk contracts, machine-parts credits, standards and durable inventory. Penitent exchange ceremonial obligations, constrained offerings, time slots and verified consecration services as well as physical items; obligations must be enforceable through game escrow with no real-money/real-life contracts. Begotten exchange cultures, growth substrates, genetic compatibility and refrigerated/incubated transport services, requiring live inventory viability. A single generic auction UI can list terms, but may not erase per-faction stage and cargo mechanics. A base can specialize and trade without surrendering territory: faction friendship is not permission to edit another player's facilities.

## 8. Gates and honest implementation boundaries

- **M0**: seeded `CityMiasma` geometry and five-seed coverage/tunneling tests. **Committed as planning geometry**; actual flight collision/ordnance and purple smoke rendering gated on streamed GPU/software world.
- **C0**: `CraftingEraRegistry` schema v2, `CrudeStructureIndex` proxy metadata, farming/survival processes and owner whitelist. **Committed as data**; living farms, plots, meshes and interactions not present.
- **U0**: `EquipmentUpgrades` to level 50 with increasing resource/time/upkeep gates. **Committed as a plan generator**; actual entity upgrades, inventory debits and persistence not live.
- **T0**: three `FactionTradeNetworks` and `SettlementSupplyBalance` data models with tests. **Committed as planning/data**; verified lots, economic simulation, contracts, multi-crew delivery and AI trade not live.
- **M1/C1/U1/T1**: wire these to world streaming, authoritative character IDs, wilderness claim geometry, actual colliders, planted crops, power networks, construction phase entities, 3D rendering, real timed production, inventory receipts and chunk restarts.
- **M2/C2/U2/T2**: two-client and multiplayer soak tests proving impossible city air raid, complete crude survival base, upgrade 1→50 without duplication, and sustained max-defense fortress powered locally but supplied by two or more external settlements.

**Do not claim** these source/data tests certify actual GPU smoke, player flying, real crafting/farming, commissioned defense towers, persistent cross-base trading or end-to-end multiplayer. Each must pass its own runtime release gate.
