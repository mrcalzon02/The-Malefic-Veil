# THREE ARCHITECTURAL LANGUAGES OF MADNESS
**The Malefic Veil — faction architecture and horror-facility art bible v1.0 — 21 October 2026**

**DESIGN/AUTHORING SPEC, NOT SHIPPING ART.** This defines the world construction language, required source parts, animations, gameplay interactions, lighting/audio and PSX visual acceptance for the three playable factions. **The Lost are hostile NPCs, not another construction faction.** Faction service tiers, costs, corpse-loss rules, size/clearance and world protection are shared; these architectural identities are **materially different authored modular building families**, not reskins.

## The readable silhouette test

At 320×200 internal rendering resolution, silhouette and material distribution must identify a faction from 60–100 meters in daylight, at night in fog, and without HUD icons. No asset may be described as implemented until an exact licensed source ID or separately authored integrated game mesh exists. Verify from actual GPU captures on GitHub, not generated visualizations. Include simple LoD0/LoD1/LoD2 silhouette budgets, reduced shaders and conventional collision primitives. All building types require named foundation, walls, corners, portals, vertical supports, roof/arch module and damaged variant; this is a **minimum kit**, not a request to model an isolated centerpiece while filling the rest with primitive gray walls.

| Faction | Structural silhouette | Material distribution | Color/light | Ambient sound, distance cue | Negative design rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Unraveled** | horizontal, irregular survivor structures; bolted cabins, water tanks, shipping-container towers, patchwork gantries and crane arms | salvaged steel, plywood, patched tarps, cordage, utility glass, dull concrete, improvised cable | rust, teal rescue markings, sodium or emergency yellow lights, cool white lamps | transformer hum, rattling sheet steel, radio crackle, uneven hammer blows | not sleek military tech, not cheerful clean prefab suburbia |
| **The Penitent** | hostile, deliberately designed **slasher-cult nightmare compounds**; crooked rooflines, enclosed catwalks, false exits, industrial stacks, murder-camp cabins, ritual stages and dead-end towers | stained wood, soot-black industrial metal, rotted canvas, hanging chains, nailed shutters, rusted instruments, iconography of the Weeping Eye | sick yellow boiler glow, red emergency lamps, sickly violet haze, asymmetric strobe | out-of-tune bell, dragging metal, furnace pressure, footsteps and whispered choir | **not** simply pristine medieval churches/cathedrals, not plain generic human houses; not a copy of specific licensed slasher film characters/buildings |
| **The Begotten** | huge organic arches forming a rib cage around **salvaged metal machinery**; tall angled spires, towering pillars, skeletal bridges, layered sacs and fleshy buttresses | blackened beams and steel frames **draped in flesh and fused bodies**, tendon cables, membrane curtains, exposed gear-assemblies under living tissue, keratin shells | deep bruised violet, veiny bone white, poisonous pearlescent highlights, bioluminescent organs | resonant breathing, fluid percussion, metal strain, distant wet tissue movement | **not** just generic alien slime texture painted on a rectangular human shed; must show strong steel-and-flesh material contrast |

**Body treatment:** Begotten structures incorporate stylized low-poly human/monster forms fused into the framework—torsos forming buttresses, silhouettes behind membrane panels, motionless figures in pillars—not loose decorative gore stickers. The arching columns should look like **huge engineered skeletons captured by living anatomy**: two or three storeys high for industrial facilities, with believable structural support and real passages/colliders. Use procedural *placement/assembly* of authored low-poly modular body/steel parts; do not procedurally substitute primitive geometry for an unmade asset pack. Limited repetitive torso/limb modules are acceptable when carefully rotated, scaled and combined into shapes that vary architecturally by facility. Rigging, animation and ownership require genuine source/provenance checks; do not reuse unrelated PSX monster models as static 'bodies' without auditing licenses/UV and meaning.

The Penitent combine **human serial-killer and murderous-cult** imagery. Every horror element is an architectural behavior, not merely bloody decoration: false safe-haven façades conceal processing rooms; deep boiler corridors route mechanical pressure to dread conduits; isolated cabins funnel players into narrow but escapable lines; lookout towers broadcast distressed ritual voices. Maintain fair PvP readability and counters: no invisible kill teleport, unwinnable fall cages, off-screen instant-kill traps or map-geometry clipping.

The Begotten **do not replace metal with flesh**: they **conquer** it. The key heroic visual is a salvaged metal machine or arch still mechanically legible, warped and dressed in sheets of tissue, tangled cable-tendons and fused figures. Their industrial structures are especially tall, with recognizably distinct load-bearing steel, anatomical growth, open space below the arch, legitimate vehicle entrances and breakable support pieces. Their death monument is a landmark, not a generic cavern.

## Terror extraction: Penitent specialization, not a PvP grief exploit

**Terror** is a faction-specialized **non-transferable, bound facility reagent called DREAD_IMPRINT**, *not* money, food, water, free weapons, or a new corpse-return loophole. The Penitent construct **Dread Works**, **Listening Towers** and **Halls of False Exits** to collect and process terror. At T1/T2 a collector can stabilize a genuine imprint from dangerous **server-verified horror encounters** involving city Lost ruins, nighttime monstrosity incursions, or a successfully completed defended ritual under active hostile pressure. It takes time to bank a raw imprint back at a working Penitent base; it is lost like any carried object on death. A powered Dread Works converts banked imprints into limited ward fuel, ritual reagents or short-lived warning effects with a throughput cap. Not an endlessly producing passive resource.

**Core anti-farm rules:** a collector does not generate imprint from merely standing near friendly players, intimidating a consenting teammate, alt account farming, repeated death loops, torturing incapacitated players, trapped/spawn-protected NPCs, empty building ambient screams, or AFK operation. Once-per-encounter credit is tied to server `threatEncounterId` + participating character + site + rolling cooldown; require active movement/engagement/survival and material reagents. Provisional economy: one imprint max per meaningful Lost site encounter per participating character, per-site 15-minute diminishing-return lock, per-base buffer 12, Dread Works processes at most four hourly while powered and staffed. Exact values are balance candidates and must be tested under 2–16 players; avoid making the horror aesthetic dependent on players terrorizing real teammates.

**Contested extraction:** while actively extracting, the Penitent's bell rings visibly/audibly within radius; opponents may destroy the collector or interrupt the channel with damage. Losing a collector destroys its unbanked imprint, not the inventory of any nearby player. Distinct horror lab facilities produce powerful *but strictly bounded* situational warning effects or ward tools, never perfect wallhack vision or infinitely stacking debuffs. The Unraveled match the utility envelope through fuel/radio and manufactured electronics; the Begotten through metabolites and resonance. Different production method, comparable utility cost/risks.

## Begotten biomass and organism machinery

Organic overgrowth consumes **NUTRIENT_STOCK** from legitimate wilderness plant/creature resources and crafted chemical feed, plus truck-delivered **METAL_STOCK** and **MACHINE_PARTS** for the actual mechanical cores. They do not generate arbitrary free flesh mass or material from player deaths. A T1 digestor processes controlled biomass to tissue-building reagent; a T2 Heart Engine pumps it through named conduits to powered facilities. If nutrients or energy fail, membrane lights darken, machine performance declines and fleshy shutters may close—but public access paths and server collider authority still behave deterministically. Damage to a strut visually tears away membrane panels exposing the steel underneath. This is **not a license to harvest other players' inventory** or to raise construction from their corpses without permissions and costs.

A Begotten owner must still unload both urban cargo types and fund industrial work, the same as every other faction. A high-level root spire may be taller and stranger than an Unraveled mast, but its collision footprint, defensibility, claim limits and raid windows are balanced by actual physical geometry.

## Modular mesh and assembly requirements

Every faction must have:
- **Foundation/transition kit:** 1×1, 2×2, 2×4 and 4×4 ground panels with explicit collision/texture seams, slope correction, foundations and terrain embed depth. All placement must occur on valid wilderness, never inside city boundaries.
- **Structural kit:** post, structural beam, corner, wall, damaged wall, window, narrow door, industrial door, gate, roof, stair/ramp, girder/rib arch, railing, large and small pipe/conduit, cable/rope/tendon segment, service vent, workbench machine base.
- **Decoration/interactable kit:** lights/lamp emitters, movement-capable machinery, stocked/empty storage, fenced yard props, faction emblem, construction phase scaffolding, service NPC point (if supported), faction-specific debris and damaged transition.
- **State set:** under construction → unpowered/inactive → operational → damaged → ruined. Source asset meshes and collider masks update consistently, including during raid destruction and repair; avoid visually functioning workstations whose crafting code refuses operation.
- **Scale targets:** T0 one floor, T1 workshop 1–2 floors, T2 industrial setpiece 2–3 floors, T3 command/research landmark 3+ floors, T4 graveyard larger monumental skyline. These are architectural intentions; exact height/geometry constrained by terrain and performance budgets.
- **Kenney source policy:** audited CC0 Retro Urban modules may be selectively reused for the Unraveled's scrap steel and as visible, transformed mechanical cores of Penitent and Begotten buildings. The Penitent's horror-house shell, ritual traps, boiler details and the Begotten's organic arches/bodies **are not presumed to exist** in that kit. Author and license-check those new meshes before a 'functional, fully modeled building' claim. Avoid reusing the PSX Characters archive as unlicensed environmental geometry.
- **PSX GPU proof:** scene uses real GPU batching and depth, nearest-neighbor source textures, vertex/membrane LOD. Large arches must retain silhouette at long distance without showing detailed body geometry every frame. Instanced standard girder/rib segments share meshes; close-up body modules have bounded draw count. Mark region ambient lights for night visibility and avoid unbounded shadow casters. Test on a real 320×200 framebuffer at street, 40m, 100m and midnight.

## Nonvisual identity and gameplay restrictions

- **Territory:** all player bases in wilderness. The city is prebuilt, immutable and owned by the world itself; no Penitent scary-cabin exploit can spawn slasher traps on a city sidewalk.
- **The Lost** continue inhabiting their own city ruin spawn structures. Some look like killers or monstrosities, but they are not **The Penitent**, who are a playable social faction of homicidal/cultist survivors.
- **Death:** all carried items lost, unless an already operational monumental graveyard seals one body in a costly owner-only escrow. Unraveled cemetery, Penitent House of Last Confession and Begotten Ossuary have identical reclaim rules, upkeep, large size and infrastructure prices.
- **PvP:** terrifying aesthetics and audio tell a story but cannot violate hitbox parity, user-set flashing/fog accessibility, audio clarity or safe spawns. Penitent false doors cannot silently mislead the game about interactability (visual fake entrance is fine, actual use should disclose locked/false on interaction). No instant kill on opening a door. Begotten fleshy walls have real blocking masks, not invisible raycast exceptions.
- **Offline raids:** same publicly visible, enforceable raid-vulnerability and structure persistence rules for all factions. No soul-banking trick grants extra protected hours.

## Art acceptance sequence

1. Author **one T0 claim anchor** from each faction; confirm dramatically different silhouettes at source 320×200 and real collision.
2. Author **one T1 workshop and one storage facility** per faction with connected door/interactable animation, source mesh IDs and material behavior (Unraveled metal/tarp, Penitent camp/boiler horror, Begotten steel beneath body membrane).
3. Author **one T2 large industrial hall** per faction including resource input, output, power failure and damage states. The Begotten hall must have great arching metal columns visibly draped in bodies; the Penitent hall must have functional fear-harvesting equipment; Unraveled hall must look scavenged rather than modern-futuristic.
4. Author **one monumental T4 graveyard** per faction with actual 24×24 footprint, stages, corpse-slot interaction and unique architecture. Use the shared death/escrow rules; no cheaper monster resurrection.
5. Acquire **GitHub Actions actual in-game screenshots** of each 3-faction comparison in day/night and above. Record source assets, draw calls, triangle counts, collision coverage and animation state. No AI concept image may be mislabeled an in-engine render.
6. Show 3 crews operating facilities with two or more clients under bandwidth/load limits; verify one faction cannot obtain a free crafting, storage, death or claim privilege through visual implementation.
