# Bastion Panel mechanism study: rejected pending repair

Independent Unraveled wall study: twenty rigid overlapping armor tiles in five columns/four vertical stages, nested vertical sleeves, drive cassette and four folding ground shoes. Horizontal travel precedes vertical extension; depth movement finishes last. Form shading/AO is baked into the 256 px atlas. Export omits normals, tangents and normal maps.

r1: 4,388 triangles; retained as the first over-budget study. r2 reduces repeated bolts and exports 3,748 triangles, still above the 3,500 target. Packed runtime envelope: 0.460 x 0.490 x 0.522 m, with minimum deployment floor height 0.0083 m. The last dimension still exceeds the packing target.

The exported-state test passes coordinate metadata, absent normal attributes/maps, nine unique-channel clips, texture assignment, deformation/transform evaluation, sampled deployment floor clearance and host-clock destruction pose selection. For this wall it checks deployment motion instead of accepting decorative motion as proof of operation. Purposeful operation and distinct failure motion remain unimplemented.

**Clearance FAIL:** 600 intersecting pair samples across 31,540 directed armor-tile pair samples: 96 during deployment and 504 in ACTIVE. Reverse-direction pair checks are counted separately. PACKED had no reported tile intersections. Equal depth offsets on diagonally adjacent tiles allow their overlapping edges to occupy the same plane in the completed wall. This is not acceptable continuous cover construction. The next repair must assign compatible lap depths and verify the full deployment again.

The lateral slide bars are construction placeholders: their physical nesting, guides, locks and load path have not been established. They must be repaired before any feasibility certification. Tile-versus-tile checks do not prove other part clearance. Structural continuity, collider/silhouette agreement, art finish and live-world integration remain unverified. No GPU review or production promotion is claimed.

Sources and exact hashes are retained in build/candidates/field-bastion-r1 and field-bastion-r2. Current evidence: reports/field-bastion-r2-clearance.json and reports/field-bastion-r2-packing.json. Design stopped when the account reported 51% used / 49% remaining in the selected five-hour allowance; this rejected study remains in the 17 deployables requiring redesign.
