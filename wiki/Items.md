## Acquisition, handling and provenance

`METAL_STOCK` and `MACHINE_PARTS` are **truck-only pallet cargo in the present live city hauling system**, not backpack-stuffable items. The server handles durable yard depletion, damaged-truck ejection and pickup. Full wilderness delivery and the base storage loop are **not live**. Raw forage, stone, timber, herbs and water have separate harvesting foundations. Dirty wetland water is not potable until purified.

Every item references its immutable catalog ID. See [asset index](https://github.com/mrcalzon02/The-Malefic-Veil/blob/main/assets/game/crafting/icons_index.csv) and [source icon manifest](https://github.com/mrcalzon02/The-Malefic-Veil/blob/main/assets/game/crafting/icons_manifest.json); rights/packaging rules remain in [assets/SOURCES.md](https://github.com/mrcalzon02/The-Malefic-Veil/blob/main/assets/SOURCES.md).
