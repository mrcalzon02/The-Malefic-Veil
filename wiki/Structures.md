Facility durations, footprints and utility demands are blueprint specifications.

**Construction constraint:** all generated world-owned structures and **all urban lots, including OPEN plots**, are forbidden to player construction. New player facilities belong only in authorized wilderness claims when that fully streamed construction system becomes available. The existing city arena's turret/healing/revival/barricade deployables are a separate runtime system.

## Separate deployment and upgrading mechanics

The current multiplayer arena has four existing host-authoritative deployable types: turret, healing station, revival station and barricade, with placement checks. Their costs and capabilities are not a blanket replacement for the facility system above. Expanded **level 1–50** upgrade schemes, facility limits, faction-specific structures, farming, AA defenses and power grids are design/data contracts until transactionally wired into the actual terrain and host world.
